values

Beauty can be all around us.

We need beauty now more than ever. Beauty that inspires future generations to challenge and improve the world. And we need new role models: master craftsmen, polymaths, and critical thinkers.

Beauty is vanishing

Most things that are profoundly beautiful are wrapped up in the canon of the past and in the splendor of the natural world. Why can’t we find this level of beauty in our globalized, cosmopolitan lives: in what we build, how we work, and how we treat one another?

Where can we find beauty untainted by polemical outcry, economic structure, technological mediation, or social stratification? Something beautiful in its craftsmanship, its subtlety, and its ability to inspire the deepest and most radiant feelings?

Four things worth making more of

The aesthetic > over the grotesque

With the coming infinitude of AI-made everything, from pictures and songs to products and information, everyone will consume exactly what they want, and society will tolerate eclecticism less and less. People will forget what intellectual discomfort truly feels like.

If people can’t tolerate aesthetic differences, how will people tolerate each other?

The sublime > over the spectacle

Mass production and consumption do not produce intellectual vitality; it contributes to intellectual paralysis. The live experience economy cannot catch up, and has little reason not to follow the trends of the internet and the cultural industry.

Make something worth replicating, not many replicated things.

Role models > over parasocial icons

In 1603 the Hamburg scholar Johannes Wower defined perfect polymathy as “wandering freely and with unbridled speed through all the fields of the disciplines.” Polymathy is an archetype of curiosity. It represents a decline of complacency in the status quo, and it promotes a kind of humility in the face of the unknown. These are qualities that can inspire a civilization.

As Leonardo da Vinci recommends: “Learn to see.“

Myth & epic > over conspiracy

The trivium and quadrivium, which inspired the creation of Mulvium, are all represented by mythological muses who inspired people to be creative and scientific. People will engender a greater sense of social responsibility to contribute and maintain the very beauty they are inspired by.

Intellectually mythical, pragmatically attainable

Reimagine social responsibility

Three people, each asked for something. Choose one.

the patron

Accumulated wealth should power the frontier, inexorably tied to human achievement.

There needs to be a funnel where personal wealth comes in and global impact comes out, where creative ideas and the creative process can be incubated.

the maker

Creativity turned toward problem solving, not only toward self-expression.

Through ollaboration between humanists and technologists. Scientists and engineers, artists and architects, each designing for a single intelligent whole that is greater than the sum of its parts.

the citizen

A framework of social humility, to reconcile divisiveness and engender harmony.

The hope is to help the world stop and smell the roses, one beautiful thing at a time.

Power the frontier, not the simulacrum.
