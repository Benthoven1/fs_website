MulviumSOS Mulvium Sponsorship Operating System

The software that builds an institution around a founder’s idea, and makes it known.

Atelier Plans and builds the institution Twelve agents under a Chief of Staff Grove Gives it a public voice Eight agents, directed by an account lead

1

Atelier

Where the institution is planned and built.

From a first consultation, Atelier drafts the institution’s complete plan, the blueprint. Every value says where it came from, and no work waits on an answer: an open question carries a sensible default until the founder decides.

From the blueprint, Atelier assembles a workforce of AI agents, organized like a small, real organization. Each holds only the tools its job requires and hands its drafts to a person. Choose one to see its work.

Chief of Staffturns goals into tasks and sequences the work

Research looks after evidence, and handles

  • interviews
  • literature review
  • citations
  • sources
  • memo
  • field notes
  • data sets
  • peer review

2

Grove

Where the institution finds its public voice.

Most of an agency retainer pays for hours of production. Grove’s agents do the production, an account lead directs them, and the client decides, often a month of the calendar at a single meeting. A full public relations program costs a fraction of a retainer.

Account leada person, who directs the team and presents its work

Brand looks after storytelling, and handles

  • brand voice
  • messaging house
  • boilerplate
  • founder story
  • key messages
  • style guide
  • tagline
  • visual identity

Projects we sponsor can add Grove to their sponsorship; other organizations can retain it on its own. One media directory and one awards directory, holding only public facts, serve every client, so no reporter hears from Grove too often, and each client’s work stays walled off from the rest.

3

An operating system

Atelier and Grove are parts of M.SOS, not programs on top of it.

An operating system is more than a core. It schedules the machine’s work, keeps its files, and carries what it says to the network and the screen, and every application relies on those parts. M.SOS is built the same way. Point to a layer to find its counterpart.

Because they are parts of one system, Atelier and Grove share everything beneath them: one record of the institution, one set of rules, one sponsor. A new part would join them the same way. For an organization Grove serves outside sponsorship, its own board and accounts take the sponsor’s place.

4

Agents draft; people decide.

When work reaches a step with real-world effect, the agent stops.

  • Signing a contract
  • Accepting a gift
  • Submitting a grant
  • Spending money
  • Speaking in public

  1. Draft

    An agent prepares the work.

  2. Request

    It files a request tied to the exact draft.

  3. Verify

    Every fact in public copy cites a confirmed record or source.

  4. Approve

    The founder or client approves; the sponsor too, where required.

  5. Act

    Only the approved words go out, on their scheduled day.

Founders and clients never log in to M.SOS: they meet their lead and receive finished work.
