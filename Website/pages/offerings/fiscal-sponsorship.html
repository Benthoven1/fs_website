Fiscal Sponsorship

A legal home for new charitable projects, and the expertise to build them into institutions.

A fiscal sponsor is an established charity that takes a new project under its legal umbrella. The project becomes one of the sponsor’s own programs, so gifts are tax-deductible and foundations can give, without the months and cost of forming a separate nonprofit.

Our nonprofit arm serves as that sponsor. It holds each project’s funds in separate books, accepts gifts and issues receipts, applies for grants, signs contracts, pays approved expenses, and keeps the project within the rules for charities. Founders keep leading the work day to day.

What sets us apart is the other half. A traditional fiscal sponsor offers a legal home and little else. Our for-profit arm pairs each founder with an engagement lead who, working with FSOS, turns the idea into a complete, operating institution: its plan, its programs, its funding, and its first hires.

Every founder accepts a Founder Policy that sets out who decides what. The Sponsor keeps legal and financial control; founders lead within the project’s purpose and budget; and money is spent while the project runs. If a project ends, its remaining funds pass to a charitable successor, never to an individual.
