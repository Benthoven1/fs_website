The problem isn’t a lack of ideas.

It’s the organization you have to build before you can begin: the bylaws and budgets, the permits and payroll, the grant reports and the insurance. So most ideas never begin.

fiscal sponsorship

At Mulvium, we change the norm: we build the organization beneath you.

Mulvium lends your project its nonprofit home, so it can raise money, sign contracts and grow, long before it is an organization of its own.

01 Begin now. Start as soon as the sponsorship agreement is signed, not after months of forming a nonprofit.
02 A fund of its own. Gifts and grants for your project are receipted and held for it alone.
03 The work beneath you. Mulvium signs the contracts, arranges the insurance and keeps the books. You lead the work.
