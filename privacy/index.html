Mulvium

Privacy

Effective 8 October 2026.

This policy covers the Mulvium website, its waitlist and its application. Mulvium is not yet open; The Mulvium Foundation has not yet been formed. Until it is, this information is held by Mulvium’s founder, Benjamin T. Rossen, and you can reach us at hello@mulvium.org.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your email when you join the waitlist; your answers to the short questions that follow; and, if you apply, your name, anyone leading the project with you, and what you tell us about the project.
  • How you found us: the page that sent you and any campaign tags in the link you followed, so we know which posts and partners reach founders.
  • Visits: anonymous counts of page views and of steps such as joining or applying. We use no cookies for this and do not follow you to other sites.

Why

To write to you when Mulvium opens, to read and answer your application, and to learn whether founders need what Mulvium would offer. If you agree on the application, your answers may also begin your project’s intake form, so you are not asked twice.

Who else handles it

Only the services that store our forms, send our email, schedule calls and count visits, acting on our behalf. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until we decide not to open Mulvium, when we delete the waitlist and the applications.

Your choices

  • Every email has a way to unsubscribe, and we honor it promptly.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to hello@mulvium.org.
  • We do not track you across other sites, so we treat every visit as if Do Not Track were on.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell the waitlist by email if the change is material.
