Privacy

Effective 14 October 2026.

This policy covers the Mulvium website and its waitlist. Mulvium is not yet open; The Mulvium Foundation has not yet been formed. Until it is, this information is held by Mulvium’s founder, Benjamin, and you can reach us at hello@mulvium.org.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your email when you join the waitlist; your answers to the short, optional questions that follow; and your name, if you give it.
  • Checking your email: we ask Google’s public DNS whether your email’s domain (the part after the @) receives mail. If we ask you to confirm your address, we email you a one-time code, kept for fifteen minutes, then discarded.
  • How you found us: the page that sent you and any campaign tags in the link you followed, so we know which posts and partners reach founders.
  • Visits: anonymous counts of page views and of steps such as joining or answering. We use no cookies for this and do not follow you to other sites.

Why

To check your email, to welcome you, to write to you when Mulvium opens submissions, to keep out automated and fake signups, and to learn whether founders need what Mulvium would offer.

Who else handles it

Only the services that store our forms, send our email and count visits, acting on our behalf. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until we decide not to open Mulvium, when we delete the waitlist.

Your choices

  • Every email has a way to unsubscribe, and we honor it promptly.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to hello@mulvium.org.
  • We do not track you across other sites, so we treat every visit as if Do Not Track were on.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell the waitlist by email if the change is material.
